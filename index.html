Privacy Policy for MRVA

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

MRVA ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when using the MRVA Android app.

1. Data Storage & Security

MRVA operates locally on your device. All notes, protected text, and converted PDF documents are stored entirely on your local device storage. We do not transmit, collect, or back up your data to external servers or cloud services.

2. Permissions Used

  • Storage / Media Access: Required to select images for PDF conversion and save generated PDF documents on your device.
  • Camera Access (Optional): Used only if you capture physical document photos directly within the app for PDF creation.

3. Third-Party Access & Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or data-sharing services. Your personal data is never sold or shared.
